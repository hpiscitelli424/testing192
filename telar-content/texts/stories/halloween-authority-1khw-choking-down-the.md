---
title: "Choking Down the Festivities"
---

According to Claire Smith, the Foot Patrol had developed a mission-oriented strategy based on the annual budget for enforcement activities ranging from $200,000 with the provision of up to 200 additional staff members deployed at a specific site. According to Geoff Banks, the leader of the Foot Patrol, their specific objective was to make Halloween a local celebration again, which meant reducing attendance to its pre-decade level.
